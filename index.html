Research

My research lies in human-centered intelligent operations and human–AI decision-making — developing intelligent operational systems that make better decisions by learning from human behavior, and adapting how humans and AI collaborate under uncertainty.

I am interested in operations research and sequential decision-making, machine learning and reinforcement learning, and behavioral operations management, with a shared goal of building decision support that keeps the human meaningfully in the loop.
