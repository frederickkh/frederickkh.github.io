# v56 Premium Academic Redesign

Figma concept: https://www.figma.com/design/N8mlCjdNFPQqKuSFvqAA6f

## Design read
Academic research portfolio for professors, collaborators, and technical recruiters. Editorial and research-led, but not decorative for decoration's sake.

## Principles
- Evidence before adjectives: important project metrics appear early and link back to case studies.
- One restrained accent: deep research green replaces the previous generic blue accent.
- Fewer containers: important sections use rules, spacing, and typography instead of adding more cards.
- Current work is framed carefully: early doctoral direction is presented as a direction, not as a finished dissertation claim.
- Motion is secondary to comprehension and has a reduced-motion fallback.
- Academic utility: CV, research profile, and selected reports are grouped in one document hub.

## Implemented in v56
- Home evidence strip with four report-backed metrics.
- Home Research Now section.
- Research orientation row.
- Academic Documents Hub.
- Refined navigation, accent color, focus states, responsive behavior, and card restraint.
- Publications remain hidden until real publication metadata exists.
